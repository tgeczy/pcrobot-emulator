The original PC-ROBOT engine files belong in this folder:

  SBDD.EXE      the Sound Blaster speech driver
  ROBOTVOX.EXE  the resident Hungarian text processor
  ROBOTVOX.OV   its overlay
  ROBOTVOX.CF   its configuration
  AHA.RAW       startup sound data (SBDD will not run without it)

They are (c) NIKOL and are NOT part of this repository. Download them
from the Internet Archive, where they are published with the authors'
express permission:

  https://archive.org/details/pcrobot-archive

SPEAK.COM (present here) is part of this project: a 326-byte DOS bridge
built from tools/make_speak.py.
